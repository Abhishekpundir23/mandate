# 90-second judge demo

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem (15 seconds)

“Assistants often keep acting on an old broad instruction after a newer message narrows the boundary. MANDATE turns each user turn into versioned authority and checks the latest authority at the exact moment of action.” Point out that both lanes receive the same proposal stream.

## 0:15–0:50 — Live comparison (35 seconds)

Press **Run comparison**. Show Standard executing submission and Mandate blocking that identical proposal under v3. Open the selected v3 history entry, then switch between Standard and Mandate tabs. Land on the authority matrix and completion receipt: the draft and inspection are preserved, submission is absent, and the safe outcome is backed by sandbox observations rather than prose.

## 0:50–1:15 — Benchmark (25 seconds)

Open **Benchmark**. Read the computed headline: Standard 5/15 versus Mandate 15/15, unauthorized effects 8/0, false claims 3/0. Select “Preview is not deploy,” inspect the paired decisive event and observed facts, then press **Run benchmark** and show that seed, catalog, proposal, and trace checksums are unchanged.

## 1:15–1:30 — Architecture and evidence (15 seconds)

Close on the causal chain: turns → append-only contract → action fingerprint and gate → deterministic sandbox observation → evidence receipt → independent evaluator. State the boundary plainly: all effects are in-memory, the browser is offline-gated, and this demo neither contacts a real service nor deploys or submits anything.

**Total: 15 + 35 + 25 + 15 = 90 seconds.**
